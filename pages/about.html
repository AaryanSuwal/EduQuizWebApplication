Our Story

EduQuiz started in a small dorm room in 2026. We noticed that while information was everywhere, true understanding was rare. Students were memorizing for tests but forgetting everything a week later.

We gathered a team of educators and AI engineers to build "The Lab" — our R&D center focused on one question: How can we use technology to make learning stick? The result was Nano Banana 2, our adaptive engine that has since helped over 100,000 students master complex subjects.
