Privacy Policy

Transparency is key. Here's how we protect and manage your data.

Last updated: February 2026

Information We Collect

Personal Data

When you create a profile, we store your username and avatar preferences locally on your device. If you choose to sign up for updates, we may collect your email address.

Usage Data

We track your quiz performance, streaks, and achievements to provide gamification features. This data is primarily stored locally in your browser.

How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and maintain the EduQuiz service
  • To personalize your learning experience and track progress
  • To improve our quiz content based on user performance
  • To communicate with you about updates (if opted in)
Your data is encrypted and never sold to third parties.

Data Security & Storage

We implement a variety of security measures to maintain the safety of your personal information.

Local First Architecture

Most of your progress data (streaks, scores, history) lives directly in your browser's Local Storage. This means you have full control over your data. Clearing your browser cache will reset this progress unless you have synced with an account.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

Access Request a copy of your data
Rectify Correct inaccurate info
Erase Delete your account & data

Have Privacy Concerns?

We are here to help with any questions.
